Beyond Chaos

Why Growth Destroys What It Creates and How Covenant Governance Sustains Firms in the AI Age
超越混沌：增长为何摧毁它自己创造的东西，以及契约型治理如何在 AI 时代维系企业

Manuscript Complete

Standard Academic Definition

The same chaos that fuels explosive early growth becomes the mechanism of collapse in a mature firm. Dr. Tong Yin calls this the chaos paradox, and answers it with three interlocking theories: Dynamic Driver Replacement Theory (DDRT), Core Code Theory, and the Home Model. Growth dividends buffer structural friction but never dissolve relational friction — and AI, by compressing the former while amplifying the latter, brings the inflection point forward.

Core Mechanisms
  • Product–Marketing Dividend (PMD) buffers structural friction (coordination failure, process gaps, information bottlenecks) but never dissolves relational friction (eroded trust, broken psychological contracts, perceived injustice)
  • When Governance Quality (GQ) overtakes the dividend as the primary driver, the firm reaches the Driver Replacement Inflection Point
  • AI is a friction catalyst: compresses structural friction dramatically, repairs no relational friction at all — the inflection arrives earlier and steeper
  • Management Debt: the compounding cost of trading long-term trust for short-term output
  • Mistrust Tax: annual cost of a trust deficit calculated across five layers
  • Survival Premium: the crisis-cost and recovery advantage a high-trust organisation holds (matched-pair composite case puts ratio at approximately 45:1)
What's Inside
  • Part I — The Reckoning: the chaos paradox; Performance Surface versus Core Code; quantifying Management Debt
  • Part II — The Theory: Jungle Explosion (α), Scalable Fortification (β), Enduring Governance (γ), and the mechanics of the inflection point
  • Part III — Three Paradigms in Practice: Wolf Culture (Huawei); Permanent Startup-ization (Tesla); Covenant Governance
  • Part IV — The Science: ten falsifiable DDRT propositions
  • Part V — The Blueprint: the six pillars of the Home Model
  • Part VI — Implementation: five-layer Mistrust Tax, eight-dimension Trust Audit, 180-day roadmap

Six parts, eighteen chapters, ten testable propositions.

同一种混乱，早期是增长的引擎，成熟后却成为崩溃的机制。本书称之为"混沌悖论"。殷彤博士提出三项相互嵌合的理论——动态驱动力替换理论（DDRT）、核心代码理论与家园模型——解释产品—营销红利何时失效、治理质量何时必须接棒，以及为什么 AI 会压缩结构性摩擦、放大关系性摩擦，让这个拐点提前到来。全书六部分十八章，附十项可检验命题、不信任税计算器、八维信任审计与 180 天路线图。

The Only Management Moat AI Cannot Cross

AI is commoditizing the measurable work organisations have spent decades optimising. The remaining moat is not a better productivity system but a harder asset to build: trust. Through six pillars, The Home Model turns costly, credible care into identity-level commitment. This is governance, not employee engagement.

Six Pillars
  • Hard Frame: Merit-Based Mobility, Institutionalized Innovation, Eliminating Property Managers (leaders who extract unit output instead of building capability)
  • Soft Connection: Career Sovereignty, Psychological Safety Net, Dignified Transitions
Mechanism Chain

Sustained Benevolence → Identity Fusion → Anti-Fragile Loyalty → Survival Premium

For a 5,000-person knowledge-intensive organisation: typical Mistrust Tax range of $50M–$150M annually; worked case totals $199M, roughly 25% of revenue, about $39,800 per employee.

CORE CODE

A New Theory of Human Capital Inimitability for the AI Era
核心代码：AI 时代人力资本不可模仿性的新理论

Manuscript Complete

Standard Academic Definition

The Core Code Law: what you can measure will be automated; what you cannot measure will determine survival. Using Barney's VRIN test, this book separates the codifiable Performance UI from the tacit Core Code — moral courage, non-linear intuition, and voluntary collective sacrifice. Written as a textbook with twelve chapters, six formal propositions, and six empirical research designs.

Key Concepts
  • Katrin Principle: the most valuable employee is the one your system cannot see (multiplier approximately 19:1)
  • Trust accumulates logarithmically — early investment pays disproportionately
  • Negative signals destroy trust 3–5x faster than positive signals build it
  • Crisis response governed by peer trust density, not company-wide average
  • Moral courage paradox: fusion can mobilise commitment and simultaneously make dissent feel like self-betrayal

中文摘要

核心代码法则：可衡量者终将被自动化，不可衡量者决定生存。真正无法模仿的是危机时刻才显形的道德勇气、非线性直觉与自愿的集体牺牲。卡特琳原则：最有价值的员工正是系统看不见的那一个（乘数约 19:1）。教科书体例：十二章，配学习目标、六条正式命题与完整实证研究设计。

Manuscript Complete

Standard Academic Definition

Civilisations neither decline in straight lines toward oblivion nor simply repeat on a flat wheel. They spiral — returning to earlier conditions, but at a different altitude. Central mechanism: the Sieve — sustained adversity strips away prosperity's disguises and tests which institutions possess genuine functional capacity rather than credential, rent, or performance.

Core Concepts
  • Comfortable apocalypse: decline arriving amid material abundance, cushioned by consumption, entertainment, and credit
  • Japan as the principal living example — a comfortable dark age
  • Global synchrony: "the world is, for the first time, a closed system. There is no outside."
  • Silent ascent: new capability and communities form during the trough, gaining economic independence first, then voice, then formal status
  • Capability revolution: winter can change the admission criterion itself — "under sufficient pressure, capability becomes the only ticket of admission"
