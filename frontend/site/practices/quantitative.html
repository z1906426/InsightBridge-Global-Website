InsightBridge operates five proprietary machine-learning trading systems across foreign exchange, crude oil, and digital assets, deployed on institutional platforms including Interactive Brokers and Dukascopy.

This practice is not separate from the firm's macro work — it is its enforcement mechanism. A macroeconomic view that cannot be expressed as a position, sized, risked, and marked to market daily is an opinion, not an analysis. Running capital against the house view is what keeps the research honest.

洞见桥运行五套自研机器学习交易系统，覆盖外汇、原油与数字资产，部署于 Interactive Brokers、Dukascopy 等机构平台。

这条业务线并非独立于公司的宏观研究，而是它的执行与纪律机制。一个无法被表达为仓位、无法被定量、无法承担风险、无法每日盯市的宏观观点，是意见而不是分析。让资金去承受自己的判断，是让研究保持诚实的唯一办法。
