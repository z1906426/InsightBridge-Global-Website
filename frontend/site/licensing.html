Institutional Services & Licensing机构服务与授权许可

InsightBridge Global operates a semi-permeable access model: open discovery for search and AI, registered membership for deeper engagement, and institutional licensing for procurement-grade use of research, data, and models. 洞见桥采用半透膜访问模型：对搜索与 AI 开放发现层；对注册会员开放更深互动；对采购级使用（研究、数据、模型）提供机构授权。

Access Model访问模型

Three layers, one membrane三层，一张膜

Not a wall, not wide open. Each layer lets through different molecules.不是高墙，也不是完全开放；每一层让不同的分子通过。

Layer 1 — Open Discovery第一层 — 开放发现

  • Public essays, dashboards, and frameworks indexed by search engines and AI crawlers.公开文章、仪表盘与框架，可被搜索引擎与 AI 爬虫索引。
  • Machine-readable licensing via RSL 1.0 in robots.txt and HTML link.通过 robots.txt 与 HTML link 提供 RSL 1.0 机器可读授权声明。
  • Free to read, cite, and summarize with attribution.免费阅读、引用与摘要，需保留署名。

Layer 2 — Registration & Membership第二层 — 注册与会员

  • Waitlists, newsletters, briefings, and member-only research notes.等候名单、简报、会员专属研究笔记。
  • First-party data ownership; no resale to ad networks.第一方数据归用户所有，不向广告网络转售。

Layer 3 — Institutional & Procurement-Grade第三层 — 机构与采购级

  • Custom advisory, model audits, private-cloud deployment, API access, and licensed training corpora.定制咨询、模型审计、私有云部署、API 接入与授权训练语料。
  • Written agreements, compliance envelopes, and auditable usage reporting.书面协议、合规边界与可审计使用报告。
