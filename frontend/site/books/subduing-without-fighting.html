Book Overview

"Subduing the enemy without fighting" is the most famous nine characters in Sun Tzu's Art of War — and the most deeply misread.

For twenty-five centuries this phrase has been reduced to diplomatic rhetoric, psychological warfare, court intrigue, or soft-power propaganda. Each reading touches one facet of the original, but none reaches its coldest, most mathematical, most structural core.

This book systematically reconstructs that core: it is not an aspirational slogan but a rigorous, actuarially definable, twelve-dimensionally deployable methodology for civilizational continuity across generations. Its mechanism operates on a mathematical rather than psychological plane, on a structural rather than rhetorical plane, on a long-cycle capital-management rather than short-cycle bargaining plane.

Three Core Propositions

One · An Actuarial Redefinition

Subduing without fighting can be strictly defined as a three-variable model: the Strategic Dimension Set S (twelve dimensions at the national level, eight at the corporate level), the Catch-Up Time Upper Bound T* (whose critical threshold is exceeding one generation, or 25+ years), and the Feasible Strategy Set F (whose end-state is the active removal of "initiate conflict" from the adversary's own strategic calculus). This definition converts a 2,500-year-old strategic philosophy into a quantitative framework that can be used directly in national strategy, corporate competition, and capital-management decisions.

Two · The Multi-Dimensional Deterrence Matrix

Cold War MAD covered only one dimension: nuclear force. Contemporary strategic competition has expanded to a complete twelve-dimensional matrix: Military, Financial, Industrial, Technological, Compute, Energy, Data, Talent, Organizational, Ideological, Coalitional, and Resilience. This book systematically maps the interactions and mutual-amplification mechanisms among these twelve dimensions, and provides a complete operational framework for how a nation should select its critical dimensional combination.

Three · Cross-Generational Maintenance Is the Key to Steady State

"First-mover strike" only secures the initial advantage. The true steady state depends on cross-generational maintenance — whether one can pivot to a new dimension when an existing one is caught up, enter a rising dimension ahead of others, and adapt first when market structure shifts. This proposition becomes more brutally consequential in the AI era than in any previous historical period: the stability window of a generational gap has compressed from roughly 40 years in the Cold War era to 12–24 months in contemporary AI.

本书简介

「不战而屈人之兵」是《孙子兵法》十三篇中最著名的九个字，也是被误读得最深的九个字。

在过去两千五百年里，它被误读成外交辞令、心理战、权谋计策、软实力宣传。这些解读都触及了原典的某个侧面，但都没有触及最冷峻、最数学、最结构化的那个内核。

本书系统地重建这个内核：它不是一句励志口号，而是一套可以被精算学定义、可以被十二维矩阵展开、可以被跨代际实施的文明续航方法论。它的机制不在心理层面，而在数学层面；不在修辞层面，而在结构层面；不在短期博弈层面，而在长周期资本管理层面。

三大核心命题

一 · 精算学重新定义

「不战而屈人之兵」可以被严格定义为三变量模型：战略维度集 S（在国家层面为 12 维，在企业层面为 8 维）、追赶时间上界 T*（关键门槛是超过一代人，即 25 年以上）、可行策略集 F（终局是「发起冲突」这一选项从对手的推演中被主动移出）。这套定义把两千五百年前的战略哲学，转化为可以在国家战略、企业竞争、以及资本管理决策中直接使用的量化框架。

二 · 多维威慑矩阵

冷战 MAD 只覆盖了核维度这一个单一维度。当代博弈已经扩展到军事、金融、产业、技术、算力、能源、数据、人才、组织、意识形态、联盟、韧性——十二个战略维度的完整矩阵。本书系统展示了这十二维之间的相互作用与相互放大机制，并给出一个国家如何选择关键维度组合的完整操作框架。

三 · 跨代际维护是稳态的关键

「先发制人」只解决了初始优势。真正的稳态在于跨代际维护——当一个战略维度被追平时，能否切换到新维度；当新维度崛起时，能否率先进入；当市场结构变迁时，能否率先适应。这一命题在 AI 时代变得比任何一个历史时期都更加残酷——代差稳定期从冷战时代的 40 年，缩短到当代 AI 的 12–24 个月。
