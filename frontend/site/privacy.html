Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 28, 2026  |  InsightBridge Global LLC

InsightBridge Global LLC ("InsightBridge," "we," "us," or "our") operates the website insightbridge.global. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide directly: When you contact us through our website contact form or by email, we collect your name, email address, company name, and message content.

Information collected automatically:

  • Usage data — pages visited, time on site, referral source, browser type, and device type, collected via Google Analytics.
  • Cookies — we use cookies for site preferences (theme, language), and analytics. See Section 5 for details.

Financial connection data (Stripe Financial Connections): If you connect a bank account through our platform, Stripe Financial Connections accesses your account information (account holder name, account number, institution name, current balance, and transaction history) solely to verify your account and facilitate agreed services. We do not store raw bank credentials.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to inquiries and provide consulting or advisory services.
  • To improve our website and understand visitor behavior (analytics).
  • To verify bank account information where required for service delivery (Stripe Financial Connections).
  • To comply with legal obligations.

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

We share data only with service providers who help us operate our website and deliver services, including:

  • Google Analytics — website usage analytics (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Google Translate — on-demand website translation (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Stripe — payment processing and Financial Connections (Stripe Privacy Policy)

These providers are contractually required to keep your information confidential and use it only as directed by us.

4. Data Retention

Contact form submissions are retained for up to 3 years for business correspondence purposes. Analytics data is retained per Google Analytics default settings (up to 26 months). You may request deletion at any time (see Section 7).

5. Cookies

We use the following types of cookies:

  • Preference cookies — store your chosen display theme and language (stored locally in your browser, never sent to a server).
  • Analytics cookies — Google Analytics (_ga, _gid) to measure site traffic. These cookies do not identify you personally.
  • Translation cookies — Google Translate places a "googtrans" cookie when you select a language.

You can disable cookies in your browser settings. Disabling analytics cookies will not affect core site functionality.

6. International Transfers

InsightBridge Global LLC is based in the United States. Our service providers (Google, Stripe) may process data in multiple countries. These providers maintain standard contractual clauses and appropriate safeguards for international data transfers.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your data.
  • Opt out of analytics tracking (use the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on).

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at [email protected].

8. Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision. Continued use of our website after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
