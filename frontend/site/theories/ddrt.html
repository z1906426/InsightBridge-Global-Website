STRATEGIC-MANAGEMENT FRAMEWORK战略管理框架

Dynamic Driver Replacement Theory动态驱动力替代理论

A strategic framework for modelling how organisations replace declining growth drivers with new ones without losing structural stability.组织在不失去结构稳定性的前提下,以新驱动力替换衰退驱动力的战略框架。

Definition. Dynamic Driver Replacement Theory (DDRT) models the organisational lifecycle as a sequence of dominant growth drivers — each of which inevitably decays. Long-lived organisations are those that identify the decay of a current driver early, incubate a successor driver in parallel, and effect the transition before the old driver's decline overwhelms the balance sheet. Firms that skip incubation, or that mistime the transition, experience either a growth cliff (successor not ready) or a stability loss (transition too abrupt, core code disrupted). DDRT provides the diagnostic lens and the sequencing playbook.

定义。动态驱动力替代理论(Dynamic Driver Replacement Theory, DDRT)将组织生命周期建模为一系列主导性增长驱动力的接替 —— 每个驱动力都会不可避免地衰退。长寿的组织是那些能够早期识别当前驱动力的衰退、并行孵化后继驱动力、并在旧驱动力的衰退压垮资产负债表之前完成过渡的组织。跳过孵化或错判过渡时机的企业,要么经历增长悬崖(后继未就绪),要么失去稳定性(过渡过于突兀,核心密码被破坏)。DDRT 提供了诊断视角与排序方法论。

Four Pillars of the Framework框架的四大支柱

Driver Diagnosis驱动力诊断

Identifying which specific driver has been carrying organizational growth — and how mature it is on its decay curve.

识别哪个具体的驱动力承载了组织增长 —— 以及它在衰退曲线上的成熟度。

Successor Incubation后继孵化

Structured investment into candidate successor drivers 5-10 years before the current driver's expected decline.

在当前驱动力预期衰退前 5-10 年,对候选后继驱动力进行结构化投资。

Transition Sequencing过渡排序

The precise handoff choreography that transfers organizational weight from old driver to new without balance-sheet shock.

精确的交接编排,将组织重心从旧驱动力转移到新驱动力,而不引发资产负债表冲击。

Core-Code Preservation核心密码保存

Ensuring the transition changes the driver, not the covenantal identity of the organisation.

确保过渡改变的是驱动力,而非组织的契约性身份。

Frequently Asked Questions常见问题

Is DDRT similar to the Innovator's Dilemma?DDRT 与《创新者的窘境》类似吗?

It shares the diagnostic premise that mature businesses struggle to transition. But DDRT is more sequencing-focused: it treats the transition itself as the strategic problem, not merely disruption resistance. It also incorporates Core Code Theory to distinguish which parts of the business must remain invariant during the transition.

它与《创新者的窘境》共享一个诊断前提:成熟企业难以过渡。但 DDRT 更聚焦于排序:它将过渡本身视为战略问题,而不仅是抵御颠覆。它还结合了核心密码理论,以区分过渡期间业务的哪些部分必须保持不变。

Where has DDRT been applied?DDRT 已在哪些领域应用?

Published applications include hospitality operators transitioning from OTA-dependent revenue to direct-booking + AI-driven pricing, mid-sized sovereign tourism authorities transitioning from resource-tourism to experience-tourism, and family enterprises transitioning from founder-generation drivers to institutional systems.

已发表的应用包括:酒店运营商从依赖 OTA 的收入过渡到直接预订 + AI 驱动定价;中型主权旅游机构从资源型旅游过渡到体验型旅游;家族企业从创始一代驱动力过渡到制度化系统。

How long does a driver-replacement transition take?驱动力替代过渡需要多长时间?

For mid-cap enterprises: typically 5-8 years from incubation to full handoff. For sovereign entities and civilisational-scale reforms: 20-40 years. Firms that attempt transitions in under 3 years usually fracture their core code — a Management Debt outcome DDRT is designed to prevent.

对中型企业:从孵化到完全交接通常需要 5-8 年。对主权实体与文明尺度的改革:20-40 年。试图在 3 年内完成过渡的企业通常会破坏其核心密码 —— DDRT 正是为防止这种管理负债结果而设计。
