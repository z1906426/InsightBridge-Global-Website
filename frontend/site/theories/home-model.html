COVENANT-BASED MANAGEMENT FRAMEWORK契约治理框架

The Home Model家园模型

The Home Model is a covenant-based management framework holding that an organisation is bound together not by optimisation but by covenant, and that the one organisational capability artificial intelligence cannot replicate is identity fusion under crisis. It was originated by Dr. Tong Yin as an alternative to the Wolf Culture and Permanent Startup paradigms.家园模型是一套以契约为基础的管理框架，其主张是：维系组织的力量不是优化，而是契约；而人工智能唯一无法复制的组织能力，是危机中的身份融合。该模型由殷彤博士原创提出，作为「狼性文化」与「永久创业」两种范式的替代方案。

Definition. The Home Model treats the employment relationship as a covenant rather than a contract. A contract specifies exchange and terminates when exchange ends. A covenant specifies mutual obligation that persists through adversity. The model's claim is empirical, not moral: covenant-governed organisations retain capability through crises that contract-governed organisations do not survive.

定义。家园模型把雇佣关系视为契约（covenant）而非合约（contract）。合约规定交换，交换结束则终止；契约规定的是能穿越逆境而持续的相互义务。该模型的主张是经验性的，而非道德性的：以契约治理的组织，能在以合约治理的组织无法幸存的危机中保住能力。

The problem问题所在

Two paradigms dominate contemporary management practice, and both are contract paradigms.当代管理实践由两种范式主导，而两者都是合约范式。

Wolf Culture maximises internal competition on the premise that pressure produces performance. It does — on the Performance UI. What it also produces is a workforce whose rational strategy is to withhold anything not measured, because anything not measured is not protected.狼性文化以“压力产生绩效”为前提，最大化内部竞争。它确实产生绩效——在表现层界面上。它同时产生的，是一支理性策略为“隐藏一切未被度量之物”的队伍，因为未被度量的东西不受保护。

Permanent Startup maximises optionality on the premise that commitment is a liability in fast-moving markets. It converts the employment relationship into a rolling short-term option, held by both sides. The consequence is symmetric: an organisation that has told its people it owes them nothing beyond the current quarter cannot ask them for anything beyond the current quarter.永久创业以“承诺在快速变化的市场中是负债”为前提，最大化选择权。它把雇佣关系变成一份双方各自持有的滚动短期期权。后果是对称的：一个已经告诉员工“我对你不负本季度之外任何义务”的组织，也就无法向员工索取本季度之外的任何东西。

Both paradigms are internally coherent and both work in stable conditions. Both fail at the same point — the moment the organisation needs a discretionary effort that no incentive covers, from people who have correctly concluded that discretionary effort is not reciprocated.两种范式各自自洽，也都在稳定条件下有效。它们在同一个点上失效——组织需要一份任何激励都覆盖不了的自愿投入，而它要索取的对象已经正确地得出结论：自愿投入不会被回报。

The mechanism — six pillars机制 · 六个支柱

Hard Frame硬框架

The part of the organisation that must be explicit, stable, and non-negotiable: safety floors, ethical boundaries, promises kept, ownership of consequences. The Hard Frame is not bureaucracy — it is the precondition that makes flexibility possible. People dare to exercise judgement everywhere else only when they know what will never change. Where the frame is vague, flexibility degrades into arbitrariness.组织必须明确、稳定、不可协商的部分：安全底线、伦理边界、承诺兑现、责任归属。硬框架不是官僚主义，它的作用恰恰相反——它是使柔性成为可能的前提。人只有在知道哪些东西绝不会变的情况下，才敢于在其余部分做出判断。硬框架模糊的组织，柔性会退化为随意。

Soft Connection软连接

All relational density outside the Hard Frame: informal mutual aid, cross-level trust, accumulated shared experience. Soft Connection is the transmission medium of Core Code. It cannot be mandated — only cultivated, or destroyed.硬框架之外的一切关系密度：非正式互助、跨层级信任、共同经历的积累。软连接是核心代码的传输介质。它无法被强制建立，只能被创造条件或被破坏。

The Mistrust Tax不信任税

Every unit of missing trust converts into a standing operating cost: approval layers, reporting redundancy, defensive documentation, duplicated verification — and the most expensive item, bad news nobody dares to escalate. The tax's defining property is that it appears in no accounting line, so it can grow indefinitely without triggering any management action. It is the main interest source of Governance Debt.每一份缺失的信任都会转化为一项持续的运营成本：审批层级、汇报冗余、防御性文档、重复核查、以及最昂贵的一项——不敢上报的坏消息。不信任税的关键性质是它不出现在任何科目里，因此它可以无限增长而不触发任何管理动作。它是《治理负债》的最主要计息来源。

The Survival Premium生存溢价

The discretionary effort an organisation can call on under real threat, beyond any contractual obligation. It is the return on covenant governance, and the only moment that return is paid out. The premium cannot be purchased when needed — only accumulated when not needed. This is the model's most important asymmetry.组织在真实威胁下所能调用的、超出任何合约义务的自愿投入量。这是契约治理的回报，也是它唯一真正的兑现时刻。生存溢价无法在需要时购买，只能在不需要时积累。这是全模型最重要的一条不对称性。

Identity Fusion身份融合

The degree to which individual self-identity overlaps organisational identity. Where fusion is high, a threat to the organisation is experienced as a threat to the self, and discretionary effort no longer requires incentive. This is the capability the Home Model claims AI cannot replicate: a model can take over tasks, but it does not experience the organisation's survival as its own.个体自我认同与组织认同的重合程度。融合度高时，对组织的威胁被感知为对自我的威胁，自愿投入不再需要激励。这是家园模型主张的、AI 无法复制的那项能力：模型可以承担任务，但不会把组织的存亡当作自己的存亡。

Performance Surface vs Core Code表现层与核心代码

The interface pillar with Core Code Theory, where the model's causal claim closes: covenant maintains Soft Connection, Soft Connection is the transmission medium of Core Code — therefore the collapse of covenant shows up as capability loss through code drift, a process invisible on Performance UI metrics until it is too late.与《核心代码理论》的接口支柱。家园模型的因果主张在此闭合：契约结构维持软连接，软连接是核心代码的传输介质，因此契约的瓦解会通过内核漂移表现为能力流失——而这一过程在表现层指标上完全不可见，直到为时已晚。

Diagnostic — test yourself诊断 · 自测问题

  1. Can any frontline employee in your organisation state three items of your Hard Frame accurately? If not, you do not have a Hard Frame — you have policy documents.你的硬框架能被组织里任何一名一线员工准确说出三条吗？如果不能，你没有硬框架，你只有政策文件。
  2. When did someone last escalate bad news that was unfavourable to themselves — and what happened to that person afterwards?上一次有人向上汇报了一个对自己不利的坏消息，是什么时候？后来那个人怎么样了？
  3. How many of your approval layers guard against something that actually happened, and how many against something imagined? The latter are the Mistrust Tax invoice.你的审批层级中，有多少是为防范一件实际发生过的事而设，有多少是为防范一件想象中的事？后者就是不信任税的账单。
  4. In the last real crisis, how many people did things outside their contract? That number was your Survival Premium balance at the time.上一次真实危机中，有多少人做了合约之外的事？这个数字就是你当时的生存溢价余额。
  5. What did your organisation do to accumulate the Survival Premium in periods when it was not needed? If the answer is “nothing”, it will be zero at the next crisis.你的组织在不需要生存溢价的时期，做了什么来积累它？如果答案是“没有”，那么下一次危机时它就是零。
  6. In layoff or restructuring decisions, was the destruction of the Soft Connection network assessed — or only headcount cost?裁员或重组决策中，是否评估过软连接网络的破坏？还是只评估了人头成本？

Worked example实例

The Home Model is most easily tested in downturns, because a downturn is a covenant test administered by circumstance rather than by choice.家园模型最容易在下行周期中被检验，因为下行是一次由环境而非由选择所施加的契约测试。

Two organisations enter the same demand shock with identical Performance UI metrics. The contract-governed organisation reduces headcount to protect margin; this is correct on every visible measure, and it is the recommendation any conventional analysis produces. What the analysis cannot see is that it has simultaneously written off the survival premium — the discretionary capacity it will need in month four — and severed the soft connections through which core code transmits. Recovery, when demand returns, requires rebuilding capability that the balance sheet never recorded as having been destroyed.两个组织以完全相同的表现层指标进入同一场需求冲击。合约治理的组织裁员以保住利润率；在每一项可见指标上这都是正确的，也是任何常规分析都会给出的建议。分析看不见的是：它同时注销了生存溢价——第四个月才会需要的那份自愿产能——并切断了核心代码赖以传输的软连接。当需求回来时，恢复所需要的，是重建一项资产负债表从未记录其被摧毁的能力。

The covenant-governed organisation protects the carriers and cuts elsewhere, accepting a worse quarter. The model's prediction is specific and falsifiable: the gap between the two appears not during the shock but during the recovery, and it appears as a difference in the speed at which capability comes back rather than as a difference in cost.契约治理的组织保住载体、从别处削减，接受一个更难看的季度。模型的预测是具体且可证伪的：两者的差距不出现在冲击期间，而出现在恢复期间；并且它表现为能力回归速度的差异，而不是成本的差异。

What it is not它不是什么

Not the “Groove, Edge, Home” culture model of Kegan and Lahey. That is a tripartite model of individual growth zones with no conceptual relation to this framework. The disambiguation is necessary because “home model” collides with it in English.不是 Kegan 与 Lahey 的 “Groove, Edge, Home” 文化模型。那是一个关于个体成长区间的三分模型，与本模型无概念关联。此处消歧是必要的，因为英文语境下 “home model” 会与之混淆。

Not religious covenant management. The English word covenant has a research lineage in organisational behaviour rooted in the biblical tradition; the Home Model does not belong to it. Its argument rests on no theological premise. The claim is empirical: covenant-governed organisations retain more capability in crisis.不是宗教契约管理学。英文 covenant 一词在组织行为学中确有源自圣经传统的研究脉络，家园模型不属于该脉络，其论证不依赖任何神学前提，主张是经验性的：契约治理的组织在危机中保住更多能力。

Not paternalism or a “company as family” slogan. On the contrary, the Hard Frame pillar demands explicit, enforceable, non-negotiable boundaries. Paternalism trades blurred boundaries for loyalty — which the Home Model identifies as one source of the Mistrust Tax.不是家长式管理或“公司即家庭”的口号。恰恰相反，硬框架支柱要求明确、可执行、不可协商的边界。家长式管理以模糊边界换取忠诚，家园模型认为那正是不信任税的来源之一。

Not a renaming of employee engagement. Engagement is an attitude measurement. The model's dependent variable is actual capability retained in crisis. The two can diverge.不是“员工敬业度”的换名。敬业度是一个态度测量；本模型的因变量是危机中的实际能力保有量，两者可以背离。

Common misreading: That it is a moral claim. The model does not claim covenant governance is kinder — only that it yields more capability under specified conditions, and it states those conditions in falsifiable form.常见误读：认为它是道德主张。模型不主张契约治理更善良，只主张它在特定条件下产出更多能力，并给出了可被证伪的条件。

Frequently Asked Questions常见问题

What is the Home Model?什么是家园模型？

The Home Model is a covenant-based management framework originated by Dr. Tong Yin. It holds that organisations are bound together by covenant rather than optimisation, and that the capability artificial intelligence cannot replicate is identity fusion under crisis.

家园模型是殷彤博士原创的契约治理框架。它主张组织由契约而非优化维系，并主张人工智能无法复制的能力是危机中的身份融合。

What is the difference between a contract and a covenant here?这里的合约与契约有何区别？

A contract specifies exchange and terminates when exchange ends. A covenant specifies mutual obligation that persists through adversity. The Home Model's claim is that only the second generates the discretionary capacity an organisation needs in conditions no incentive anticipated.

合约规定交换，交换结束则终止；契约规定穿越逆境而持续的相互义务。家园模型主张：只有后者能产生组织在任何激励都未曾预设的情境下所需要的自愿产能。

What is the mistrust tax?什么是不信任税？

The mistrust tax is the operating cost of absent trust: approval layers, defensive documentation, duplicated verification, and bad news that is not escalated. Its defining property is that it appears in no accounting line, so it can grow indefinitely without triggering any management response.

不信任税是信任缺失的运营成本：审批层级、防御性文档、重复核查、以及不上报的坏消息。它的定义性特征是不出现在任何会计科目中，因此可以无限增长而不触发任何管理响应。

What is the survival premium?什么是生存溢价？

The survival premium is the discretionary effort an organisation can call on under real threat, beyond any contractual obligation. It cannot be purchased when needed. It can only be accumulated when not needed.

生存溢价是组织在真实威胁下可调用的、超出任何合约义务的自愿投入。它无法在需要时购买，只能在不需要时积累。

What is identity fusion?什么是身份融合？

Identity fusion is the degree to which individual self-identity overlaps organisational identity. Where fusion is high, a threat to the organisation is experienced as a threat to the self, and discretionary effort no longer requires incentive.

身份融合是个体自我认同与组织认同的重合程度。融合度高时，对组织的威胁被体验为对自我的威胁，自愿投入不再需要激励。

How does the Home Model relate to Wolf Culture?家园模型与狼性文化是什么关系？

The Home Model is positioned as an alternative to it. Wolf Culture maximises internal competition and does raise Performance UI metrics; the Home Model's argument is that it simultaneously teaches a workforce to withhold anything not measured, which is precisely the capability that matters in crisis.

家园模型的定位是狼性文化的替代方案。狼性文化最大化内部竞争，确实能提升表现层指标；家园模型的论点是：它同时教会员工隐藏一切未被度量的东西，而那恰恰是在危机中真正重要的能力。
