What does "three altitudes" mean?「三个高度」是什么意思？

It means the same analytical method is applied at three levels of resolution: national strategy for sovereign entities and ministries, industry strategy for sectors and institutional investors, and operational strategy for individual enterprises. What is The Home Model?什么是家园模型？

The Home Model is a covenant-based organisational governance framework originated by Dr. Tong Yin. It holds that management is not optimisation but covenant, and that the organisational capability artificial intelligence cannot replicate is identity fusion under crisis. Its pillars include Hard Frame / Soft Connection, the Mistrust Tax, the Survival Premium, Identity Fusion, and the distinction between Performance Surface and Core Code.家园模型是殷彤博士原创的、以契约为基础的组织治理框架。它主张管理不是优化而是契约，人工智能无法复制的组织能力是危机中的身份融合。其支柱包括硬框架／软连接、不信任税、生存溢价、身份融合，以及表现层与核心密码之辨。

What is Governance Debt?什么是治理负债？

Governance Debt is a diagnostic framework originated by Dr. Tong Yin for identifying structural organisational decline. It treats unresolved governance and trust deficits as accumulating liabilities that impose compounding costs on an organisation's future capacity, in the same way financial debt imposes interest.治理负债是殷彤博士原创的、用于识别组织结构性衰退的诊断框架。它把未解决的治理与信任赤字视为不断累积的负债，像金融债务的利息一样，对未来组织能力复利计息。

What is Dynamic Driver Replacement Theory?什么是动态驱动力替代理论？

Dynamic Driver Replacement Theory (DDRT) is a strategic framework originated by Dr. Tong Yin that models organisational lifespan through the succession of the drivers that sustain an organisation. It explains why organisations decline when a founding driver is exhausted and no successor driver has been installed.动态驱动力替代理论（DDRT）是殷彤博士原创的战略框架，通过维系组织的驱动力的更替来建模组织寿命。它解释了为什么当创始驱动力耗尽、而接续驱动力尚未就位时，组织会走向衰退。
