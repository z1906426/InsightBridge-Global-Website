Research Brief研究简报

Why weighted demand beats static rules为何加权需求优于静态规则

Executive summary: Rule-based revenue management applies fixed if-then rate rules (e.g. "raise rates on weekends") that ignore real-time demand. AI dynamic pricing instead learns the relative weight of each demand driver and re-prices continuously, capturing revenue that static rules leave on the table — especially during compressed holiday and event windows. In live InsightBridge Global deployments this approach delivered a 6–12% ADR uplift and reduced effective OTA commission share by 3–5 percentage points. 执行摘要: 基于规则的收益管理使用固定的「如果—就」定价规则(例如「周末涨价」),无法响应实时需求。AI 动态定价则学习每个需求驱动因子的相对权重并持续重新定价,捕捉静态规则错失的收入——尤其在节假日和活动导致的需求压缩窗口。在 美国洞见桥全球公司 的真实部署中,该方法实现了 6–12% 的 ADR 提升,并将有效 OTA 佣金占比降低了 3–5 个百分点。

How it works运作原理

Each demand driver contributes to an aggregated demand index. That index lifts the room rate above the base, up to (but never beyond) the ceiling guardrail. RevPAR is then projected at the chosen occupancy. 每个需求驱动因子贡献于一个聚合需求指数。该指数将房价从基础水平向上推高，但永远不会超过上限护栏。RevPAR 则基于您选择的入住率投影计算。
Honest scope. The driver weights and the demand-aggregation math execute server-side and are not disclosed. This calculator demonstrates the shape of POLARIS's logic — the production engine uses an XGBoost + LSTM hybrid trained on additional signals, plus a full guardrail system (GM approval, competitor-deviation limits, price floors/ceilings). Figures here are illustrative, not a forecast for any specific property. 诚实声明。需求因子权重与聚合数学逻辑在服务器端执行，不对外公开。本计算器展示 POLARIS 逻辑的形态——生产版引擎采用基于更多信号训练的 XGBoost + LSTM 混合模型，并配有完整护栏系统(总经理审批、竞品偏离限制、价格下限/上限)。此处数值仅为示意,不构成对任何具体物业的预测。
