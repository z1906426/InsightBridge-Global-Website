Bridging two decades of global operations and frontier AI research. 连接二十余年全球运营经验与前沿 AI 研究。

Dr. Tong Yin is a management scholar, strategic analyst, and quantitative systems developer who serves as Founder, & Chief Scientist of InsightBridge Global LLC and its sister research entity, InsightBridge Global Lab LLC. His work bridges more than twenty-five years of international executive experience with advanced research in artificial intelligence, revenue optimization, and organizational governance.

殷彤博士是管理学者、战略分析师与量化系统开发者,担任 InsightBridge Global LLC 及其姊妹研究实体 InsightBridge Global Lab LLC 的创始人兼首席科学家。他的工作将二十五年以上的国际高管经验,与人工智能、收益优化和组织治理领域的前沿研究相衔接。

Career prior to InsightBridge 创立 InsightBridge 之前的职业经历

Before founding InsightBridge, Dr. Yin served as an Instructor at Auburn University while completing his Ph.D. in Hospitality Management. Over the preceding two decades he held senior executive positions with international firms across hospitality, professional services, and global manufacturing in the United States, China, Australia, and Europe — leading operations, commercial strategy, and cross-border expansion for organizations at scale.

创立 InsightBridge 之前,殷博士在攻读酒店管理博士期间任奥本大学讲师。此前二十年间,他曾在美国、中国、澳大利亚与欧洲的国际企业中担任酒店业、专业服务与全球制造业的高级管理职务——主导规模化运营、商业战略与跨境扩张。

Present ventures 当前业务

InsightBridge Global LLC is an AI-driven revenue optimization and strategic advisory firm serving the global hospitality and financial sectors. The firm engineers autonomous dynamic-pricing systems that reduce hotels’ dependency on OTAs, advises mid-sized sovereign entities and regional governments on tourism master plans across the Middle East, Southeast Asia, and Europe, and deploys machine-learning trading systems on institutional platforms including Interactive Brokers and Dukascopy. The company also publishes InsightBridge Global Intelligence, a bilingual data-backed briefing distributed across the hospitality sector.

InsightBridge Global LLC 是一家以 AI 驱动的收益优化与战略咨询公司,服务全球酒店与金融行业。公司自主研发动态定价系统以降低酒店对 OTA 的依赖;为中东、东南亚与欧洲的中型主权实体及区域政府提供旅游总体规划咨询;并在 Interactive Brokers 与 Dukascopy 等机构级平台上部署机器学习交易系统。公司同时发行双语数据型简报《InsightBridge Global Intelligence》,覆盖全球酒店业。

InsightBridge Global Lab LLC, established in 2026 and based in Auburn, Alabama, is an independent research-only entity prototyping long-horizon AI and AI-driven robotics for hotel operations, guest services, and international tourism infrastructure over the coming decade.

InsightBridge Global Lab LLC 成立于 2026 年,总部位于美国阿拉巴马州奥本市,是一家独立的纯研究实体,专注为未来十年的酒店运营、宾客服务与国际旅游基础设施原型开发长周期 AI 与 AI 驱动的机器人技术。

Research and theoretical frameworks 研究与原创理论框架

Dr. Yin’s scholarly work centers on organizational resilience, AI strategy, geopolitical risk, and civilizational governance. He is the originator of several theoretical frameworks widely referenced in his field: The Home Model, a covenant-based governance framework grounded in human-centric traits AI cannot replicate; Management Debt, a framework for diagnosing structural organizational decline; and Dynamic Driver Replacement Theory together with Core Code Theory, strategic frameworks for modeling organizational lifespan and stability. He is a regular editorial contributor to Hospitality Net, Hotel News Resource, and Phocuswire.

殷博士的学术工作聚焦于组织韧性、AI 战略、地缘政治风险与文明治理。他是多个业界广泛引用的原创理论框架的创建者:家园模型(The Home Model)——一个以 AI 无法复制的人本特质为根基的契约型治理框架;管理负债(Management Debt)——诊断组织结构性衰退的分析框架;以及动态驱动力替代理论(DDRT)与核心密码理论(Core Code Theory)——建模组织生命周期与稳定性的战略框架。他亦是 Hospitality Net、Hotel News Resource 与 Phocuswire 的固定编辑撰稿人。
