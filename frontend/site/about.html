Present ventures 当前业务

InsightBridge Global LLC is a theory-driven strategic think tank. Unlike advisory firms that license borrowed frameworks, InsightBridge originates its own: The Home Model, Governance Debt, Dynamic Driver Replacement Theory, and Core Code Theory, developed across nine academic manuscripts — with Subduing Without Fighting: Applications in the AI Era as the strategic master outline of the corpus — six of them under contract or review with Routledge, CABI, Columbia University Press, Cambridge University Press, and Bloomsbury Academic. The firm applies these frameworks at three altitudes — national strategy for sovereign entities and ministries, sector strategy for industries and institutional investors, and operational strategy for enterprises — and builds the AI systems that carry strategy into execution. It runs three practices:

  • Strategic Planning & Advisory — national strategy and economic-diversification master plans, sovereign programme stress tests, industry structural analysis, and enterprise governance strategy;
  • Applied Vertical AI — industry-specialised large-model systems, delivered through the POLARIS, ORION and NOVA total-revenue optimisation systems and the research entity InsightBridge Global Lab LLC;
  • Quantitative Trading Research — five proprietary machine-learning trading systems across foreign exchange, crude oil, and digital assets, deployed on institutional platforms including Interactive Brokers and Dukascopy (proprietary capital only; nothing on this site constitutes investment advice).

The firm publishes InsightBridge Global Intelligence, a weekly bilingual strategic-intelligence briefing written for sovereign wealth funds, central banks, ministries, and institutional investors.

Research and theoretical frameworks 研究与原创理论框架

Dr. Yin’s scholarly work centers on organizational resilience, AI strategy, geopolitical risk, and civilizational governance. He is the originator of four management frameworks: The Home Model, a covenant-based governance framework grounded in the human-centric capabilities AI cannot replicate — most centrally, identity fusion under crisis; Governance Debt, which treats unresolved governance and trust deficits as liabilities compounding against an organisation’s future capacity; Dynamic Driver Replacement Theory, which models how an organisation exchanges an exhausted growth driver for a new one; and Core Code Theory, which separates an organisation’s codifiable Performance UI from its tacit, identity-based Core Code. His research has received 26 verified citations across 7 countries and 5 languages. He is a regular editorial contributor to Hospitality Net, Hotel News Resource, and PhocusWire.

Between May and July 2026, Dr. Yin published dated, attributed forecasts on Saudi Arabia’s Vision 2030 tourism programme and graded them against a strict rule: a forecast counts only where the deciding evidence was published after the forecast. On 13 May he argued the average-rate decline was structural rather than cyclical — eight weeks later GASTAT recorded −11.4%. In the same article he identified the holy cities as an inelastic exception — five weeks later Knight Frank recorded Hajj-week Makkah RevPAR at +39%. On 6 July he called de-hotelisation at NEOM — eight days later NEOM rewrote its website. The full archive is at Track Record.

殷博士的学术工作聚焦于组织韧性、AI 战略、地缘政治风险与文明治理。他是四套管理框架的原创者：家园模型——以人工智能无法复制的人本能力（其核心为危机中的身份融合）为根基的契约型治理框架；治理负债——把未解决的治理与信任赤字视为对未来组织能力复利计息的负债；动态驱动力替代理论——建模组织如何以新驱动力替换已耗尽的旧驱动力；核心密码理论——区分组织中可编码的表现层界面与内隐的、基于身份的核心密码。其研究已获得 26 项已核实引用，横跨 7 个国家与地区、5 种语言。他亦是 Hospitality Net、Hotel News Resource 与 PhocusWire 的固定专栏作者。

2026 年 5 月至 7 月，殷彤博士就沙特 Vision 2030 旅游计划公开发表了一组具名、具日期的判断，并按严格标准逐条打分——只有当决定胜负的证据发表于预测之后，该项预测才计入：5 月 13 日预判平均房价下跌属结构性而非周期性，八周后由沙特统计总局确认为 −11.4%；同文预判圣城为刚性需求例外，五周后由莱坊确认麦加朝觐周 RevPAR +39%；7 月 6 日提出 NEOM「去酒店化」，8 天后 NEOM 重写官网。完整档案见预测战绩。
