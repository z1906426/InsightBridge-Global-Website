Published Forecasts, Scored Against What Actually Happened公开发表的预测，按真实发生的结果逐条打分

A think tank is only worth the record it is willing to publish. This page lists forecasts made by InsightBridge Global before the confirming data existed, with the publication date, the original article, the subsequent official statistic, and the independent source for that statistic. Where a forecast was wrong, imprecise, or where the confirming evidence turned out to predate the call, that is stated here too.一家智库的价值，等于它愿意公开的记录。本页列出洞见桥在印证数据出现之前所作的预测，附发表日期、原文链接、后续官方统计数字，以及该数字的独立信源。若某项预测有误、不够精确，或印证证据其实早于预测本身，同样在此列明。

We hold ourselves to one rule: a claim only counts if the evidence that settles it was published after the forecast was. Analysis that arrives after the fact is commentary, not foresight.我们只守一条规则：只有当决定胜负的证据发表于预测之后，这项预测才算数。事后抵达的分析是评论，不是预判。

Record 01 — Saudi ADR decline is structural, not cyclical记录 01 — 沙特 ADR 下跌是结构性的，而非周期性的Confirmed已印证

Forecast published预测发表日13 May 2026
Original article原文Why Vision 2030 Hotels Need More Than Traditional Revenue Management — Hospitality Net
Data available at time of writing撰文时可得数据Q4 2025
Settling data published裁决数据发表July 2026 (GASTAT Q1 2026 tourism statistics)2026 年 7 月（沙特统计总局 Q1 2026 旅游统计）
Interval间隔~8 weeks周

What was claimed. "A 12 percent ADR decline — the exact decline Saudi hospitality recorded in Q4 2025 — wipes 4 million SAR off the top line in a single year… Saudi Arabia is now entering the phase that every great hospitality boom eventually meets: the moment when the pace of new supply outruns the speed at which traditional revenue management systems can adapt to a fundamentally different demand profile."

The load-bearing word is structural. The consensus reading at the time treated the Q4 2025 softness as cyclical — a geopolitical demand dip that would mean-revert. The forecast held that it was a supply-absorption effect driven by roughly 23,600 new rooms per year, and would therefore persist and deepen rather than revert.

What subsequently happened. Saudi Arabia's General Authority for Statistics reported Q1 2026 hotel ADR at SAR 423, down 11.4% year-on-year from SAR 477, with occupancy at 60.8% against 63.0% a year earlier — reported independently by Arab News, Argaam and AGBI. Forecast magnitude and measured magnitude differ by less than one percentage point. Licensed hospitality facilities rose 22.7% to 6,122 units over the same period — the supply mechanism the forecast named as the cause.

The methodological objection, and the answer to it. A second dataset reads the opposite way, and it should be addressed rather than omitted. STR-benchmarked figures reported by Arab News in May 2026 show national Q1 2026 occupancy at 66.3% with ADR up 3.0% to SAR 805.5. Both datasets are accurate. They differ in sample: STR benchmarks the branded, chain-affiliated existing stock; GASTAT is a census of all licensed hospitality facilities.

That difference is the entire point. A supply-absorption effect appears in the census and is invisible in the branded panel, because the inventory doing the absorbing — the 22.7% growth to 6,122 licensed facilities — sits largely outside the branded sample. An operator reading only the branded benchmark in Q1 2026 would have concluded rates were rising. The forecast was about the total market's capacity to absorb supply, and the census is the instrument that measures it.

2026 年 5 月 13 日，基于 2025 年四季度数据，判断沙特酒店 ADR 约 12% 的同比跌幅属结构性而非周期性，成因是每年约 23,600 间新增客房超出市场吸收能力。7 月沙特统计总局公布 Q1 2026 实际 ADR 为 423 里亚尔，同比下跌 11.4%，误差不足一个百分点；同期持牌住宿设施增加 22.7% 至 6,122 家，正是预测所指认的成因。另有 STR 口径显示 ADR 上涨 3.0%——两组数字都真实，差别在样本：STR 只统计品牌连锁存量，GASTAT 统计全部持牌设施。供给吸收效应只在普查口径中可见，这恰恰是该预测成立的机制。

Record 02 — The holy cities are the exception the framework predicted记录 02 — 圣城是框架事先预言的例外Confirmed已印证

Forecast published预测发表日13 May 2026
Original article原文Why Vision 2030 Hotels Need More Than Traditional Revenue Management — Hospitality Net
Settling data published裁决数据发表21 June 2026 (Knight Frank KSA Hospitality & Religious Tourism Report)2026 年 6 月 21 日（莱坊沙特酒店与宗教旅游报告）
Interval间隔~5 weeks周

What was claimed. The analysis separated elastic leisure demand from inelastic institutional demand, and identified pilgrimage as the region's only genuinely fortress-like segment — meaning the supply-driven rate compression forecast for the Kingdom as a whole should not appear in Makkah and Madinah.

What subsequently happened. Knight Frank's June 2026 KSA Hospitality & Religious Tourism Report recorded Makkah as the Kingdom's strongest-performing hospitality market. During Hajj week Makkah RevPAR rose 39% at an average room rate of US$861, while Madinah occupancy held at 82% — against the national ADR decline of 11.4% over the same period.

Why this record matters more than the first. A framework that only predicts decline is a pessimism generator. This entry is included because the same model that forecast the national rate collapse simultaneously forecast where the collapse would not occur, and named the mechanism — demand inelasticity — in advance. A framework is only credible if it predicts its own exceptions.

同一篇 5 月 13 日的文章区分了弹性休闲需求与刚性机构需求，判定朝觐是本地区唯一真正堡垒式的细分市场，因而全国性的供给压价不应出现在麦加与麦地那。莱坊 6 月报告显示：朝觐周麦加 RevPAR 上涨 39%、平均房价 861 美元，麦地那入住率维持 82%——同期全国 ADR 下跌 11.4%。一套只会预测下跌的框架是悲观情绪发生器；这套框架同时预测了自己的例外，并事先说明了机制。

Record 03 — "Functional repurposing" of the mega-project layer记录 03 — 超级项目层的"功能重置"Confirmed已印证

Forecast published预测发表日6 July 2026
Settling event裁决事件14 July 2026
Interval间隔8 days天

What was claimed. That the rational path was no longer to defend the original ultra-luxury vision but to pursue orderly absorption of oversupply — including what the analysis termed "de-hotelisation": converting surplus inventory toward institutional uses, and repurposing the mega-projects themselves around infrastructure logic rather than leisure fantasy. The underlying argument was that mega-projects require a realistic demand curve, and the ultra-luxury tourism strategy did not have one.

What subsequently happened. On 14 July 2026, NEOM rewrote its public-facing website, stripping out the futuristic renderings and the sweeping promises that had defined The Line's original pitch — a quiet abandonment of the leisure-fantasy framing in favour of an infrastructure narrative. Eight days after the call.

What is deliberately excluded from this entry. Two frequently cited data points are not claimed as confirmations here, because they predate the forecast:

  • The US$16 billion earmarked for NEOM contract terminations was reported by Semafor on 7 June 2026, a month before the 6 July article.
  • The reduction of The Line's 2030 population target to below 300,000 was first reported by Bloomberg in April 2024, and construction was effectively suspended in September 2025.

Both are relevant context for the retrenchment. Neither is evidence of foresight, and neither is counted as such.

2026 年 7 月 6 日提出"去酒店化"——把过剩存量转向机构用途，把超级项目本身按基础设施逻辑而非休闲幻想重新定位。8 天后的 7 月 14 日，NEOM 悄然重写官网，删除 The Line 的未来主义渲染与承诺，转向基础设施叙事。

本条刻意排除两项常被引用的证据：160 亿美元合约取消（Semafor 6 月 7 日报道）与 The Line 人口目标下调至 30 万以下（彭博 2024 年 4 月首报），二者均早于该预测，不计为预判成绩。

Record 04 — Riyadh would absorb the shock first记录 04 — 利雅得将最先承受冲击Not scored不计分

Forecast published预测发表日13 May 2026
Mark标记Not scored — confirming data predates the forecast不计分——印证数据早于预测

What was claimed. That new supply creates a structural "cold-start problem" — a revenue management system without historical comparables cannot price a market that has not yet revealed its demand curve — and that the capital, carrying the heaviest pipeline, would therefore feel the compression earliest and most sharply.

Why it is not counted. Q1 2026 city-level data showing Riyadh occupancy down 13.5 percentage points to 52.2% with RevPAR down 9.5% is consistent with the forecast. But JLL published its KSA Q1 2026 hospitality market dynamics on 7 May 2026 — six days before the article appeared. Under this page's scoring rule, evidence available before publication cannot be claimed as confirmation, regardless of whether it informed the analysis.

The cold-start argument remains open. It will be scored against Q3 and Q4 2026 city-level data when published.

预测利雅得因管线最重而最早、最深地承受压力。Q1 2026 城市级数据（利雅得入住率下降 13.5 个百分点至 52.2%、RevPAR 下降 9.5%）与预测一致，但仲量联行的该份报告发布于 5 月 7 日——早于文章 6 天。按本页规则，发表前已存在的证据不得计为印证。此项留待 2026 年三、四季度城市级数据裁决。

Why publish the miss alongside the hits为什么把不计分的一条并列公开

Three of these four forecasts are counted as confirmed. One is not, because the evidence that would have supported it arrived first. We publish that entry with the same prominence as the others, because a record that only lists its successes is a marketing page, not a track record.

InsightBridge publishes this archive not for vindication but for method. Industries that cannot distinguish diagnosis from reassurance keep making the same capital-allocation errors. A dated, sourced, independently verifiable archive is where that distinction lives.

These forecasts were made in hospitality and tourism because that sector publishes government statistics against which a forecast can be scored. The frameworks that produced them are not sector-specific. → From Industry Proof to General Method
